Visage - Image Handler

Checks images against the my.wbs upload limits and fixes the ones that need it. Runs entirely in your browser, nothing is sent anywhere.

Version 1.0

my.wbs limits

  • Rejected only if over 500 KB and over 1024 px wide or tall.
  • Over 500 KB and too big: compressed to under 500 KB at full size. Only shrunk if compression alone can't get there.
  • Over 6500 px on either side: reduced to 6501 px and compressed.
